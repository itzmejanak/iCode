RaTeX draws iCode's math (docs/markdown-rendering-plan.md). Shipped: its native library
libratex_ffi.so 0.1.15 for arm64-v8a, unchanged (third_party/ratex), and iCode's binding to it
and drawing of its layouts, adapted from RaTeX-CMP 0.1.15. The KaTeX fonts it draws with are
under the SIL Open Font License: OFL-KaTeX.txt.

Below the two, the Rust crates linked into the library (cargo tree for aarch64-linux-android,
without build-time macros). The release records no crate versions (no Cargo.lock); each text is
from the version its sources resolve to (2026-10-21).
